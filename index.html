Research Interests & Technical Focus

Beyond my academic curriculum, I am deeply interested in the research of advanced mathematical models and the theoretical foundations of Artificial Intelligence. My core areas of investigation include:

  • Statistical Mechanics & Deep Learning: Analytical explanation of neural networks and understanding their learning processes through statistical physics.
  • Mathematical Modeling & PDEs: Study and application of Partial Differential Equations (PDEs) within AI models.
  • Signal Processing & Denoising: Exploration of SOTA generative approaches operating in the time-frequency domain, with a specific focus on denoising techniques.
